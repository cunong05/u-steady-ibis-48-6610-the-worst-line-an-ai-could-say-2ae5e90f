A single pickup line, the worst one an AI could plausibly deliver: flat,
over-helpful, and faintly ominous. The machine scans your face, books the
wedding before saying hello, and asks you to confirm like a calendar invite.

> I analyzed your face for 0.4 seconds and have already scheduled our wedding. Please confirm.

Run it: `python3 main.py` — writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
